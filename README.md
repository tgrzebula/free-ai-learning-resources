## AI

### CS50’s Introduction to Artificial Intelligence with Python (Harvard, 2020)
- [Course Website](https://cs50.harvard.edu/ai/)
- **Topics:** core ideas behind modern AI (search, knowledge, uncertainty, optimization, learning, neural networks, language)
- Use the link above for free lessons and free certification. A Google search result will usually redirect you to edX, where there are some limitations and the certificate is paid.
- The lectures are from 2020, but the general AI topics are still up to date.
- 7 lectures (about 2 hours each) + 12 Python projects

### CS221: Artificial Intelligence: Principles and Techniques (Stanford, Autumn 2025)
- [Course Website](https://stanford-cs221.github.io/autumn2025/)
- [Lectures on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rMeDqwS1yFl3j3sR_-MQNEN)

### MGMT298D: Science and Strategy of Artificial Intelligence (UCLA Anderson, Spring 2026)
- [Course Website](https://ucla-anderson-ssai.github.io/SSAI/)
- 10 week MBA. 3 ML + 4 DL + 3 Building LLM Apps
- For non-technical students, but includes some technical tasks
- **Lack of video** lectures

### 1-hour Introduction to AI by Prof. Malan - part of CS50’s Introduction to Computer Science
- [Lecture Video & Materials](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/weeks/ai/)

## Deep Learning

### 6.S191 Introduction to Deep Learning (MIT, 2026)
- [Course Website](https://introtodeeplearning.com/)
- 9 Lectures, 3 Labs

### 15.773 Hands-on Deep Learning (MIT, Spring 2024)
- [MIT OpenCourseWare Link](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-773-hands-on-deep-learning-spring-2024/)
- 11 lectures, 2 homeworks
- Sloan School of Management

### CS230: Deep Learning (Stanford, Autumn 2025)
- [Course Website](https://cs230.stanford.edu/)
- [Lectures on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rNRRGdS0rBbXOUGA0wjdh1X) - newer than the Fall 2018 videos linked on the course website
- Taught by Andrew Ng and others
- **Topics:** neural networks, CNNs, RNNs, supervised and self-supervised learning, adversarial robustness, generative models, deep reinforcement learning, AI project strategy
- 9 lectures
- Especially interesting: [Lecture 8 (Agents, Prompts, and RAG)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1njvbBmfsw) and [Lecture 9 (Career Advice in AI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AuZoDsNmG_s)

## LLMs

### CME295: Transformers & Large Language Models (Stanford, Autumn 2026)
- [Course Website](https://cme295.stanford.edu/)
- [Lectures on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLakGfbKCei80) - ongoing
- [Autumn 2025 lectures (complete)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rOCXd21gf0CF4xr35yINeOy)
- **Topics:** Transformers, LLMs, LLM training, reinforcement learning with LLMs, LLM systems, AI agents, LLM evaluation, diffusion LLMs, trending topics
- 9 lectures, no homework (midterm + final exam only)

### 10-202: Introduction to Modern AI (CMU, Spring 2026)
- [Course Website](https://modernaicourse.org/)
- [Lectures on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@modernaicourse)
- **Topics:** supervised learning, PyTorch, neural networks, self-attention, Transformers, KV caching, tokenization, LLM training, post-training and chat tuning, reinforcement learning, RLHF and reasoning models, AI safety
- 24 lectures (19 with video; AI safety and AGI lectures not published), 8 homeworks (from training a linear model to implementing a minimal LLM)
- Free online version with autograded homeworks (Colab or marimo notebooks)

## AI Agents

### 11-768: AI Agents (CMU, Fall 2026) ⭐
- [Course Website](https://www.cmu-agents.com/)
- [Lectures on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLSN0qpDfUvTM) - ongoing
- Co-taught by an OpenHands co-founder
- **Topics:** tool use, context management, skills and memory, planning and multi-agent coordination, coding agents, computer use agents, deep research agents, SFT and reinforcement learning, agent safety and monitoring, frameworks (OpenHands, LangGraph), human-agent interaction, search (critic models, tree search)
- 23 lectures (including guest lectures), 3 assignments + team research project

### CS329Z: Engineering AI Agents (Stanford, Fall 2026)
- [Course Website](https://cs329z.stanford.edu/)
- [Lectures on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLPRcF5HR02_U) - ongoing (lecture 1 published on 2026-11-10)
- Co-taught by a SWE-bench co-creator and a DSPy core contributor
- **Topics:** LLMs for builders, RAG, tool use and function calling, agent frameworks and design patterns, memory architectures, multi-agent systems, optimization, data for agentic systems, evaluation and benchmark design, LLM-as-judge, agent safety and guardrails, coding agents, proactive agents
- 19 lectures, 2 assignments + project
- [Unofficial AI-generated video walkthroughs on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLVKEyLMx8AmU) - **Not the real lectures**, only AI narration of the public slides; a stopgap while the official playlist fills up
- [Unofficial notes on Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/Stanford-CS329Z-Engineering-AI-Agents-40bbeb410d4a81508326e66407927ed1)

### CS2680: Modern AI Systems: Agents and System Optimizations (Harvard, Fall 2026)
- [Course Website](https://cs2680.seas.harvard.edu/)
- **Topics:** how Claude Code works, multi-agent workflows, agent design (context and tools, multi-agent architecture, memory and reliability, evaluation), cost and performance, agent safety, LLM serving (batching, speculative decoding, prefix caching, KV-cache, quantization, routing)
- 25 sessions, 5 assignments (from a web UI for Claude Code to serving and optimizing your own agent) + final project
- Slides available
- **Lack of video** lectures
- [Unofficial AI-generated video walkthroughs on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLCGg5We_BF7w) - **Not the real lectures**, only AI narration of the public slides

## AI Engineering

### Machine Learning in Production / AI Engineering (CMU, Spring 2026)
- [Course Website](https://mlip-cmu.github.io/s2026)
- [Lectures on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLDS2JMJnJzdmubSKnanmIwzr08cionWm_) - 24 lectures
- **Topics:** turning prototypes into production systems with ML, LLMs and agents: risk and safety, testing ML pipelines, deployment and monitoring, A/B tests and canary releases, data quality and drift, scaling, versioning and reproducibility, technical debt, LLMs, RAG and agents (incl. MCP)
- Free [book](https://mlip-cmu.github.io/book/)
- 4 assignments, 11 labs + team project


## Agentic Coding / Agentic Software Engineering

### CS146S: The Modern Software Developer (Stanford, Fall 2026) ⭐
- [Course Website](https://themodernsoftware.dev/)
- **Topics:** internals of coding agents, context engineering, spec-driven development, MCP, agent skills and CLI, agent-ready codebases, agentic code review, security, background agents, AI-native teams, the software factory
- 10 weeks, slides and readings available
- 6 [assignments](https://github.com/mihail911/modern-software-dev-assignments)
- **Lack of video** lectures
- Fall 2026 guest lecture: [How always-on agents work (Lee Robinson)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L1OBRtfPS34)
- [Interview with the instructor](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wEsjK3Smovw) - course philosophy
- Previous edition (Fall 2025, complete, but ~85% of the material changed in 2026): [Course Website](https://themodernsoftware.dev/fall2025) · [8 assignments](https://github.com/mihail911/modern-software-dev-assignments/tree/fall2025)
